# Working with Misha

- Misha is the designer. When a treatment or placement is given (e.g. "use the resume tile as the example"), apply it exactly as it exists. Don't reinterpret placement, alignment, or layout to fit what seems better.
- Don't take design liberties on simple things. If something looks like it conflicts with the given treatment, ask in one line instead of adapting it.
- Keep it simple: do the obvious version first.
