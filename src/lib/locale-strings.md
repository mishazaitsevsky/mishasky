## HUMAN — 8 strings, register/idiom matter

These need a translator who understands the dry, slightly adversarial tone.
Not formal. Not polite. Terse and direct.

| key | english |
|-----|---------|
| N0_A | You can't hold the tide with a broom |
| N0_QUIP_A | Then let's find out what the tide needs. |
| N0_B_QUIP | Welcome, child of Flynn. |
| N0_C_QUIP | The only way to have time is to take it. |
| N3_C | Drilling a hole in the darkness. |
| N3_C_QUIP | A bit dramatic, but, agreed. |
| TERMINAL_EOL | end of line. |
| WIN_COLD | Could be a fit! |

Notes for translator:
- N0_A: someone charging at a problem that can't be solved by brute force
- N0_B_QUIP: Tron (1982) — "Flynn" is a known character, dub line may already exist
- N0_C_QUIP: tone is philosophical, not motivational
- N3_C: working hard on something with no clear outcome, no guarantee
- N3_C_QUIP: dry acknowledgment, not enthusiasm
- TERMINAL_EOL: Tron reference — "End of line." is a known line, check dub
- WIN_COLD: casual, slightly surprised, warm but not effusive
